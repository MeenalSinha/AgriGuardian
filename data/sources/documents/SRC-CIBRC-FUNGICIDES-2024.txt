STATUTORY NOTICE ON AGROCHEMICAL USAGE IN HORTICULTURAL CROPS:
All applications of insecticides, fungicides, bactericides, and plant growth regulators must strictly adhere to the approved doses, dilution rates, target pests, and pre-harvest intervals (waiting periods) notified under the statutory gazette. Off-label use, unverified tank mixtures, and unauthorized chemical prescriptions on vegetable crops are punishable under Sections 29 and 30 of the Insecticides Act, 1968.

APPROVED FUNGICIDES & PROTECTANTS FOR TOMATO (Pages 86-91):
1. Mancozeb 75% WP:
   - Target Disease: Alternaria solani (Early Blight), Phytophthora infestans (Late Blight)
   - Application Dosage: 1.5 - 2.0 kg/ha formulation (2.0 g/L in 750 - 1000 L water/ha)
   - Statutory Waiting Period (Pre-Harvest Interval - PHI): 7 days
   - Quote (Page 88): "Mancozeb 75% WP is officially labeled for Alternaria solani on Tomato at 1.5-2.0 kg/ha (2.0 g/L dilution) with a statutory waiting period of 7 days."
   - Toxicity Classification: Toxicity Class III (Slightly Hazardous, Blue Triangle).

2. Chlorothalonil 75% WP:
   - Target Disease: Early Blight, Anthracnose, Fruit Rot
   - Application Dosage: 1.5 - 2.0 kg/ha formulation (2.0 g/L water)
   - Pre-Harvest Interval (PHI): 7 days
   - Precaution: Broad-spectrum multi-site contact fungicide; apply thoroughly to upper and lower leaf surfaces.

3. Copper Oxychloride 50% WP:
   - Target Disease: Early Blight, Late Blight, Bacterial Spot (Xanthomonas suppression)
   - Application Dosage: 2.5 kg/ha formulation (2.5 g/L water)
   - Pre-Harvest Interval (PHI): 5 days
   - Toxicity Classification: Class III.

4. Metalaxyl 8% + Mancozeb 64% WP (Co-formulation):
   - Target Disease: Late Blight (Phytophthora infestans) systemic and contact containment
   - Application Dosage: 1.5 - 2.0 kg/ha (2.0 g/L)
   - Pre-Harvest Interval (PHI): 10 days
   - Restriction: Maximum two applications per season to prevent systemic phenylamide resistance.

BANNED & PROHIBITED SUBSTANCES IN VEGETABLES (ANNEXURE IV):
The following active ingredients are strictly prohibited on vegetable and horticultural crops:
- Monocrotophos: Banned on all vegetables due to acute oral and dermal mammalian toxicity.
- Endosulfan: Complete ban on manufacture, sale, and use pan-India per Supreme Court order.
- Paraquat Dichloride: Banned for foliar spraying on growing vegetable food crops.
- Phorate, Methyl Parathion, Carbofuran, Dichlorvos: Banned or restricted from open foliar application.

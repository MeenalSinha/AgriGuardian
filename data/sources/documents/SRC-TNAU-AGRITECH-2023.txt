SECTION 1: BIO-CONTROL OF SOLANACEOUS FOLIAR PATHOGENS (Pages 10-15)
Trichoderma Protocols (Page 12):
"Prophylactic application of Trichoderma viride formulation @ 10g/L significantly curtails early blight lesion development in field tomatoes."
Bio-control agents Trichoderma harzianum or Trichoderma viride should be applied as foliar sprays (5-10 g/L or 2.5 kg/ha in 500 L water) at the first detection of localized lower-leaf spots. Alternatively, apply 5% Neem Seed Kernel Extract (NSKE) or commercial neem oil (1500 ppm @ 2-3 ml/L) to deter fungal spore germ-tube elongation and deter piercing-sucking insect vectors.

SECTION 2: NUTRIENT-DISEASE INTERACTIONS IN FIELD CROPS (Pages 42-46)
Paddy Blast (Magnaporthe oryzae) and Nitrogen Dynamics (Page 44):
"Overuse of urea fertilizer without potash balance directly triggers virulent blast epidemics under cloudy Kharif conditions."
Excessive nitrogen top-dressing softens cuticular thickness and promotes lush, succulent leaf blade tissue. Maintain balanced N:P:K nutrition (recommended 120:40:40 kg/ha in 3 split doses). Potassium (K2O) enhances leaf silica deposition, creating physical mechanical resistance to fungal appressorial penetration.

SECTION 3: VECTOR TRAPPING & SUCKING PEST THRESHOLDS (Pages 20-24)
Whitefly (Bemisia tabaci) and Thrips in Vegetables:
Yellow sticky traps (15-20 traps/acre) placed 15 cm above the crop canopy provide early warning and continuous non-chemical suppression of alate whiteflies and aphid vectors. Blue sticky traps are specific for thrips monitoring.
